# Pricing and plan details

ArtsFolio plans define artwork capacity, mailing-list capacity, custom domain availability, and platform sales commission disclosure. Current plan details are shown on the public pricing page and in the tenant billing area.

Instagram publishing is included with Studio, Professional, and Collective. It is not available on Free.

Custom domains are included with Professional and Collective. Free and Studio use an ArtsFolio subdomain.

The public `/features` page describes the platform's portfolio, scheduled publication, Release Groups, bulk upload, Instagram, discovery, collector, sales, analytics, access, and operational features. Where a feature requires a specific plan, the requirement is shown beside it.

Free tenant pages include an ArtsFolio notification/link as part of the free plan.
